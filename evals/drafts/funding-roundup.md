# The AI infrastructure money map

Databricks, valued at $38 billion, is the company every data team benchmarks against. ChatGPT has 100 million weekly active users, which is why every product roadmap now has an assistant on it.

For a sense of how fast this moved: when Stripe raised in March 2021, it was valued at $95 billion. Nvidia first passed $1 trillion in market value in 2023. Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers.
