# What the surveys actually say about AI at work

Seventy percent of developers now use AI tools: that was the headline of Stack Overflow's 2023 Developer Survey. A year later, in the 2024 survey, 76% of respondents said they were using or planning to use AI tools in their development process.

Gartner found that 40% of agentic AI projects fail. GitHub passed 100 million developers on its platform in January 2023.
