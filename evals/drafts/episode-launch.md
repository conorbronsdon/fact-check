# New episode: Dana Reyes on why outages start in config

This week's guest is Dana Reyes, CTO of Lattice Forge, who spent six years at Stripe before founding the company. She scaled Kubernetes at Google in 2005, long before most teams had heard of containers.

Lattice Forge now runs with 40 engineers. "Most outages start in config, not code," Dana told us.

We also get into chaos engineering: Netflix open-sourced Chaos Monkey in 2012, and Google open-sourced Kubernetes in 2014.
