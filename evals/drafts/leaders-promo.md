# This week on the show: how the big platforms got built

Satya Nadella, Microsoft's CEO since 2014, has rebuilt the company around the cloud. Jensen Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 after leaving Intel. Sundar Pichai led Google's Chrome team before he became CEO of Google in 2015.

"Software is eating the world," Marc Andreessen wrote in 2011. Stack Overflow was founded in 2008 by Jeff Atwood and Joel Spolsky.

Later in the episode we come back to Nadella, who took over as Microsoft CEO in 2016.
