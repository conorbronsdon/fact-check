# Speaker lineup: Build Week keynote

Our keynote host is Mara Lindqvist, founder of Larchmere Labs. Before Larchmere she ran the data platform team at Netflix from 2018 to 2020.

While she was there, she scaled that platform to serve 300 million subscribers.

Her talk is about long careers inside one company, and she uses three well-known paths as examples. Tim Cook spent 13 years at Apple before he took over as CEO in 2011. Sundar Pichai spent 15 years at Google before becoming its CEO in 2015. Sheryl Sandberg spent a decade at Google before moving on.

Jeff Dean joined Google in 1999.
