# Agents Need Guardrails, Not Budgets | Fernwick's Priya Raman

This week's guest is Priya Raman, Head of Products at Fernwick. She explains why agent spend needs a ceiling before it needs a policy.

Fernwick routes 2 billion requests a day. Its router was trained on Nvidia's open Nematron models. The GPUs come from CoreWeave, the GPU cloud provider.

Before Fernwick, Priya worked at Kindrel, the infrastructure business IBM spun off in 2021. She says Fernwick's agents run for 30 days without a human touching them.
