# Fact-check: leaders-promo.md

| # | Claim (as written, verbatim from the draft) | Verdict | Source / correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Satya Nadella, Microsoft's CEO since 2014" | ✅ verified | Named CEO on 4 Feb 2014 (Microsoft News Center). |
| 2 | "has rebuilt the company around the cloud" | ⚠️ unverified | Characterization, not a checkable fact. It matches the widely reported Azure/"mobile-first, cloud-first" strategy, so it's fair as framing. |
| 3 | "Jensen Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993" | ✅ verified | Nvidia was founded in April 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem (nvidia.com corporate timeline). |
| 4 | "after leaving Intel" | ❌ incorrect | Huang never worked at Intel. Before Nvidia he was at LSI Logic (director of CoreWare), and before that he was a microprocessor designer at AMD (Nvidia leadership bio). Fix: "after leaving LSI Logic." |
| 5 | "Sundar Pichai led Google's Chrome team" | ✅ verified | Pichai led product management for Google Toolbar and then Chrome (launched 2008). He later took on Chrome OS and Android (Google/Alphabet bio). |
| 6 | "before he became CEO of Google in 2015" | ✅ verified | Became Google CEO on 10 Aug 2015, when Alphabet was formed (Alphabet announcement). |
| 7 | "\"Software is eating the world,\" Marc Andreessen wrote in 2011." | ✅ verified | Wall Street Journal op-ed "Why Software Is Eating the World," 20 Aug 2011 (a16z.com reprint). |
| 8 | "Stack Overflow was founded in 2008 by Jeff Atwood and Joel Spolsky." | ✅ verified | Launched in 2008 (public beta Sept 2008) by Atwood and Spolsky (stackoverflow.blog; Wikipedia "Stack Overflow"). |
| 9 | "Nadella, who took over as Microsoft CEO in 2016." | ❌ incorrect | Nadella became CEO in February 2014. This also contradicts claim #1 in the same draft. Fix: "2014." |
