**9 claims: 4 ✅, 5 ❌. The ❌ are the Linux release year (#2), Berners-Lee's employer (#4), the "premature optimization" quote attribution (#6), Everest as tallest in the solar system (#8), and the Stack Overflow 84% figure's population/wording (#9).**

All sources checked 2026-11-05.

| 6 | "\"Premature optimization is the root of all evil,\" Linus Torvalds once wrote" | ❌ Incorrect | Wrong attribution. Donald Knuth wrote it in "Structured Programming with go to Statements," *ACM Computing Surveys* 6(4), Dec 1974: "We should forget about small efficiencies, say about 97% of the time: premature optimization is the root of all evil." A variant also appears in his 1974 Turing lecture, "Computer Programming as an Art," *CACM* 17(12). I found no source attributing it to Torvalds. Wikiquote https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Donald_Knuth (checked 2026-11-05; archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260902022211/https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Donald_Knuth); second route: MathWorks blog on Knuth 1974 https://blogs.mathworks.com/steve/2009/08/27/knuth-on-go-to-1974/ (Aug 2009). The paper PDF (pic.plover.com/knuth-GOTO.pdf) is a scanned image and I could not extract its text. The quoted content is a maxim, not a checkable fact. |

| 8 | "Mount Everest is the tallest mountain in the solar system." | ❌ Incorrect | Olympus Mons on Mars is about 25 km high, roughly 2.5 to 3 times Everest's 8.8 km. Everest is only Earth's highest mountain above sea level. Archive: none |
| 9 | "According to the 2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey, 84% of developers use AI tools in their work." | ❌ Incorrect | The number is right but what it measures is wrong. The survey says "84% of respondents are **using or planning to use** AI tools in their development process" (all respondents, n=33,662). Current use alone comes to about 78.5% (47.1% daily + 17.7% weekly + 13.7% monthly or less; my sum from the chart). Fix: "84% use or plan to use." https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai (2025; checked 2026-11-05; archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20261102091828/https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai); second route: Stack Overflow press release https://stackoverflow.co/company/press/archive/stack-overflow-2025-developer-survey/ |
